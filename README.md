# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: September 25, 2026**

Welcome to **Secure EHR**. This Privacy Policy explains how Secure EHR ("we", "our", or "the application") handles information when you use our application and related services.

Secure EHR is designed to help users securely manage and access electronic health records and related healthcare information.

## 1. Information We Collect

Depending on how the application is configured and used, Secure EHR may collect or process the following types of information:

### Personal Information

* Name
* Age or date of birth
* Gender
* Contact information
* User account information

### Health Information

* Medical history
* Diagnoses
* Symptoms
* Medications
* Allergies
* Blood pressure and other health measurements
* Doctor or healthcare-provider information
* Medical reports and records
* Other information entered into the electronic health record

### Technical Information

The application may process limited technical information required for proper operation, such as:

* Device information
* Application version
* Operating-system information
* Error or diagnostic information

We only collect information that is necessary for the intended functionality of the application.

## 2. How We Use Information

Information processed by Secure EHR may be used to:

* Create and manage electronic health records
* Display health information to authorized users
* Help users organize their medical information
* Provide healthcare-related record management features
* Maintain and improve application functionality
* Protect the security and integrity of the application
* Diagnose technical problems and improve reliability

We do not use personal or health information for purposes unrelated to the functionality of Secure EHR without appropriate permission or legal basis.

## 3. Health Information Privacy

Health information is particularly sensitive. Secure EHR is designed with the intention of limiting access to health records to authorized users.

Users should avoid entering unnecessary sensitive information into the application and should keep their account credentials and devices secure.

## 4. Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information handled by Secure EHR against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

Depending on the implementation, security measures may include:

* Authentication and authorization
* Secure communication
* Access controls
* Encryption where implemented
* Secure local or cloud storage
* Protection of authentication credentials

However, no electronic storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 5. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information or health information.

Personal or health information may only be disclosed when:

* The user has authorized the disclosure;
* It is necessary to provide a requested service;
* It is required by applicable law or a valid legal process; or
* It is necessary to protect the security, rights, or safety of users or others.

If Secure EHR uses third-party services for authentication, hosting, analytics, cloud storage, or other functionality, those services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

## 6. Third-Party Services

Secure EHR may use third-party technologies or services to provide certain functionality.

Examples may include:

* Cloud hosting
* Authentication services
* Database services
* Analytics or crash-reporting services

Where third-party services are used, only the information necessary for the relevant functionality should be shared.

## 7. User Responsibilities

Users are responsible for:

* Keeping their login credentials confidential;
* Using strong passwords where applicable;
* Logging out of shared devices;
* Keeping their devices secure;
* Providing accurate information;
* Not accessing another person's health records without authorization.

Users should not use Secure EHR to access or disclose health information without the appropriate authorization.

## 8. Data Retention and Deletion

Information may be retained for as long as necessary to provide the application's functionality or to satisfy applicable legal, security, or operational requirements.

Where applicable, users may request deletion or correction of their personal information.

The exact deletion process may depend on how Secure EHR is deployed and where the information is stored.

## 9. Children's Privacy

Secure EHR is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate authorization.

If you believe that information belonging to a child has been collected improperly, please contact us so that appropriate action can be taken.

## 10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the application, its features, or applicable requirements change.

When changes are made, the **Last Updated** date at the top of this policy will be updated.

Users are encouraged to review this page periodically.
