Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 25, 2026

Welcome to Secure EHR. This Privacy Policy explains how Secure EHR handles information when you use our application and related services.

Secure EHR is designed to help users securely manage and access electronic health records and related healthcare information.

Privacy First: Secure EHR is designed to protect sensitive health information and limit access to authorized users.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features used, Secure EHR may collect or process the following types of information:

  • Name and basic profile information
  • Date of birth and demographic information
  • Contact information
  • Medical history
  • Diagnoses and symptoms
  • Medications and allergies
  • Blood pressure and other health measurements
  • Medical reports and records
  • Doctor or healthcare-provider information
  • Technical and diagnostic information required for application operation

2. How We Use Information

Information processed by Secure EHR may be used to:

  • Create and manage electronic health records
  • Display health information to authorized users
  • Organize and manage medical records
  • Provide application features and services
  • Maintain application security and reliability
  • Identify and resolve technical problems
  • Improve the functionality and user experience of the application

3. Health Information

Health information is particularly sensitive. Secure EHR is designed to restrict access to health records to authorized users.

Users should only enter information that is necessary for the intended use of the application and should not share their account credentials with unauthorized individuals.

4. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

Depending on the implementation, security measures may include:

  • Authentication and authorization
  • Access controls
  • Secure communication
  • Encryption where implemented
  • Secure local or cloud storage
  • Protection of authentication credentials

However, no electronic storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information or health information.

Information may be disclosed only when:

  • The user has authorized the disclosure.
  • It is necessary to provide a requested service.
  • It is required by applicable law or a valid legal process.
  • It is necessary to protect the security, rights, or safety of users or others.

6. Third-Party Services

Secure EHR may use third-party services for functionality such as authentication, hosting, cloud storage, analytics, or crash reporting.

Where third-party services are used, those services may process information according to their respective privacy policies.

7. User Responsibilities

Users are responsible for:

  • Keeping account credentials confidential.
  • Using appropriate security measures on personal devices.
  • Logging out when using shared devices.
  • Providing accurate information.
  • Accessing health records only when properly authorized.
  • Not sharing another person's health information without authorization.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Information may be retained for as long as necessary to provide the application's functionality or to satisfy applicable legal, security, or operational requirements.

Where applicable, users may request correction or deletion of their personal information.

9. Children's Privacy

Secure EHR does not knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate authorization.

If you believe that information belonging to a child has been collected improperly, please contact us so that appropriate action can be taken.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the application's features, services, or applicable requirements change.

When changes are made, the Last Updated date at the top of this policy will be updated.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or the handling of information by Secure EHR, please contact us.
